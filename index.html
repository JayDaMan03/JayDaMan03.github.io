Jay Stream Clips

Jay Stream Clips is a personal tool used by the streamer Jaydaman03 (Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok) to save short highlight clips from his own live streams and upload them to his own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

The tool runs only on the streamer's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Saves a 15–30 second highlight when the streamer asks for one during a live stream.
  • Uploads that clip, with a title, description and hashtags, to the streamer's own YouTube channel.
  • Sends that clip to the streamer's own TikTok drafts, where he reviews and posts it himself.
