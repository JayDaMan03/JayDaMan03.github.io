Privacy Policy – Jay Stream Clips

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Jay Stream Clips is a personal tool operated by Jaydaman03 for his own accounts only. This policy explains what data it uses.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and to update the title and description of the operator's own live broadcasts.
  • TikTok: permission to send videos to the operator's own TikTok drafts and to read basic profile info (display name) to confirm the right account is connected.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used only to upload the operator's own clips.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from any other user is collected. The tool has no other users.
  • Video clips are stored on the operator's own computer and home network storage.

Google API Services

Jay Stream Clips's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or from TikTok's Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the stored tokens on the operator's computer also removes access.
